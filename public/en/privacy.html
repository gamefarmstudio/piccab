Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Terms of Use · ภาษาไทย

This is an English translation of our Thai Privacy Policy, provided for convenience. If the two versions differ, the Thai version prevails.

In short

  • If you don't sign in, your scenes, pictures and progress stay in your browser only.
  • If you sign in, we keep your email, scenes, pictures, progress and word sets so they sync across your devices.
  • When you tap ✨ Let AI find words, that picture is sent to AI (Google's Gemini) for processing. The words you get are made by AI, not by people.
  • We don't sell data, we show no ads, and we use no third-party tracking.
  • You can delete scenes, word sets, or your account with all its data yourself in the app.

1. Who we are

Piccab (piccab.com) is a service for learning vocabulary from pictures. “Piccab” is the data controller under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA) for the data described in this policy. Contact us at contact@piccab.com.

2. Data we collect

When you use Piccab without signing in

Scenes, pictures, words, progress, word sets and settings (such as the read-aloud accent and your menu language) are saved in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). We don't receive them, except for pictures you choose to send to AI as described in section 4.

When you sign in

  • Account data: the email you sign in with, and when the account was created and last signed in.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google sends the email, name and profile picture of your Google account to our account system (Supabase). We use the email only to identify your account. We don't ask for access to anything else in your Google account, such as Gmail, Drive or contacts, and we never receive your Google password.
  • Content you create: scene pictures, scene names, words, meanings, box positions in pictures, review progress and word sets.
  • AI credits: how many AI uses you have left that month.
  • Classes: class names, nicknames in a class, and progress on assignments (see 4.2).
  • Friends (if you turn it on): nickname, friend code, practice statistics, friend list, blocks, cheers and scenes sent between friends, and reports (see 4.3).

Technical data

When you open the website, our hosting provider's systems receive basic data such as your IP address, browser type and request time. We use it to run the website, keep it secure and limit unusual use (such as calling AI too often).

Feedback, errors and usage counts

  • Feedback you send (💬 Send feedback): the message, its type, an email to reply to (if you give one), the page you were on, your browser and device, and your account if you're signed in. We use it only to read it, reply, and fix problems.
  • App errors: if the app hits an error on your device, it sends us the error message, where in the code it happened, the page you were on and your browser version. Nothing about who you are, and none of your pictures, scenes or words.
  • Daily usage counts: the app counts totals, such as how many devices opened the app that day, new devices, and how often each mode was used, and sends them as daily totals. No device id, account, picture, scene or word. We use these numbers to see which parts of the app people use and what to improve.

We use no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we serve our fonts from our own servers. Words are read aloud with the voices already on your device or in your browser.

3. How we use data

  • To provide the service you ask for: signing in with an email code or a Google account, storing and syncing scenes, pictures, progress and word sets across devices, and finding words with AI (legal basis: contract).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems (legal basis: legitimate interests).
  • To comply with the law, where we have a legal duty.

We don't sell or rent personal data, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't send marketing email. The only email we send is the sign-in code you ask for.

4. AI in the app

When you tap ✨ Let AI find words, the scene's picture is sent through our server to Google's Gemini API to find the things in it, along with English words, Thai meanings and box positions, and the result comes back to the app. Pictures are sent only when you tap this button.

  • We use the Gemini API free of charge. Under Google's terms, the pictures sent and the results may be used by Google to provide, improve and develop Google products and AI technology, and may be read by Google reviewers; Google separates the data from your account before reviewers see it. See the Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • When you tap 📝 Photo of the list — AI reads it, the photo of the word list is sent to Gemini in the same way to read the words and meanings, using 1 AI credit. We don't keep the photo; we keep only the word list you save and the word-card picture the app draws from it. Before taking the photo, cover or crop out student names, school names and other personal information.
  • The words and boxes are made by AI and may not all be correct. You can edit or delete every word.
  • When you practise another language (such as German) while signed in, the app automatically sends only the English words and Thai meanings in your scenes to AI for translation — no pictures.
  • Please don't send AI pictures showing people's faces, documents or personal information. Photograph only the things around you, because pictures you send may be used as described above.

4.1 Shared links

When you tap 🔗 Share and make a link, we keep a copy of the pictures and words in that scene or word set, as they were when you shared it, in your account. Anyone with the link can open it and save a copy on their own device. People who get the link don't see your email or name, and links can't be searched for or guessed.

  • You can cancel a link at any time under 👤 My account → Shared links. When you cancel it, we delete that link's copy of the pictures straight away (copies people have already saved stay on their devices).
  • When you delete a scene you've shared, its links are cancelled too, and when you delete your account, all your links are deleted.
  • Children and young people should share links only with people they know, such as classmates or teachers.

4.2 Classes

Teachers can create classes and assign scenes or word sets. Students join a class with the class code and a nickname.

  • Student accounts: if a student isn't signed in, we create an anonymous account in that browser, with no email or real name. This account works only in that browser and can't use AI.
  • What teachers see: the nickname the student chose and their progress on assignments (words answered correctly, words with a ★, and when it was updated). Teachers don't see students' emails, other scenes or any other data.
  • Files teachers export: teachers can download this same data (nicknames, join dates and progress) as an Excel or CSV file for grading or school reports. That file is the responsibility of the teacher and school, not Piccab.
  • What students see: the class name and assignments, but not the teacher's email. If the teacher turns on the 🏆 ranking, students in the same class see each other's nicknames and number of correct words (nothing else). If not, students see nothing about their classmates.
  • We ask that students use a nickname, not their real first and last name. Teachers should tell their school or parents before students start using Piccab.
  • Students can leave a class themselves; teachers can remove students or delete the class, and the class progress is deleted with it. When an account is deleted, all related class data is deleted.

4.3 Friends

Friends is only turned on when you tap “Start using Friends” and choose a nickname yourself.

  • No user search: you can only become friends with a friend code or QR you give out yourself, and the other person has to accept.
  • What friends see: your nickname, your 🔥 streak, and how many words you practised this week and in total. Friends don't see your email, scenes or other progress.
  • No chat: only ready-made cheers, and scenes or word sets (which the receiver must accept first), can be sent. Inbox messages are kept for 30 days.
  • Student accounts (no email) can only be friends with classmates.
  • You can remove, block or report a friend at any time. Reports are kept for the Piccab team to review (reporter, reported person, reason and time). Tapping “Turn off Friends” deletes your friend profile, friend list and inbox.

4.4 Reports and safety

  • You can report a friend, or report an inappropriate shared link (no sign-in needed to report a link). We keep the reporter (if signed in), the reported person, the link, the reason and the time.
  • When a report comes in, the Piccab team is notified by email. Our moderators look only at the data needed for the report, such as nicknames, what the reported person sent the reporter, and the reported person's shared links.
  • If we find a violation, we may delete shared links, remove someone from Friends, or suspend the account.

4.5 Speaking practice (microphone)

In 🎤 Speak mode, the app uses your browser's built-in speech recognition to turn what you say into text and compare it with the word. The microphone is only on while you tap 🎤, and your browser asks for permission first.

  • Your browser may send the sound to its maker's speech service to be processed, such as Google for Chrome or Apple for Safari, under that provider's policy.
  • Piccab never receives, passes on or keeps your voice. The app only gets the text the browser heard, and compares it on your device.
  • It uses no AI credits and needs no sign-in.

6. How long we keep data

  • Scenes, pictures, progress and word sets are kept until you delete them. When you delete a scene, its pictures are deleted from picture storage too.
  • Copies in shared links are kept until you cancel the link, delete the scene, or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete all your pictures and account data straight away (providers' backups may remain for a short time before they're deleted on schedule).
  • Feedback is kept until we've dealt with it, and no longer than 2 years. Feedback sent while signed in is deleted with your account.
  • App errors are kept for 90 days, daily usage counts for about 13 months.
  • Technical logs are kept briefly, as needed for security.

7. Children and young people

Piccab is designed for learners of every age, and you can practise without signing in. For creating an account:

  • Users under 20 (minors under Thai law) should get consent from a parent or guardian before creating an account.
  • Children under 10 must have a parent, guardian or teacher create and manage the account.
  • If a parent or guardian finds that a child has given us data without consent, contact contact@piccab.com and we'll delete it.

To keep learners safe, we instruct the AI to choose only words suitable for every age, and not to find words in inappropriate pictures.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you have the right to:

  • Access and get a copy of your data
  • Data portability (the app's 📤 send-scene-as-a-file button works straight away)
  • Correct your data
  • Delete your data: delete scenes or word sets in the app, and delete your account with all its data under 👤 My account → Delete account and all data
  • Object to or restrict the use of your data
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent
  • Complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC)

To use these rights, email contact@piccab.com from the email you sign in with. We'll reply within 30 days.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted with HTTPS. The database is set up so each account can see and change only its own data, and your pictures can only be opened when signed in to your account. Still, no system is 100% secure. If a data breach affects you, we'll inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or the law changes, and will show the new effective date at the top of this page. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app first.
