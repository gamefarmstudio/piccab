# Piccab logo files

The mark is a picture (frame, mountains and a sun) with a big letter **A** stepping out of its corner: *a picture becomes a word*. It sits on a blue-to-purple tile.

These files are not served by the site. The app's own copies are in `public/`: `icon.svg` (the tab icon), `favicon.ico`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `og-image.png`.

## Which file to use

| Use | File |
|---|---|
| Logo with name, light background (slides, documents, letters) | `piccab-logo.svg` / `.png` |
| Logo with name, dark background | `piccab-logo-on-dark.svg` / `.png` |
| App icon (rounded tile) | `piccab-app-icon.svg`, `piccab-app-icon-1024.png` |
| App stores (square; the store rounds the corners) | `piccab-app-icon-square.svg`, `piccab-app-icon-store-1024.png` |
| Very small (under 48 px): thicker lines, simpler mountains | `piccab-favicon.svg` |
| Mark without the tile, light background | `piccab-mark.svg`, `piccab-mark-1024.png` |
| Mark without the tile, dark background | `piccab-mark-on-dark.svg` |
| One colour (stamps, embroidery, print) | `piccab-mark-ink.svg`, `piccab-mark-white.svg` (the white one is for dark backgrounds) |
| Link previews and social posts (1200 × 630) | `piccab-social-1200x630.svg` / `.png` |

Prefer the SVGs: they stay sharp at any size and can be opened in Figma, Illustrator or Inkscape. The name and the A are drawn as outlines (Fredoka SemiBold), so they don't need the font installed.

## Keep it recognisable

- Keep the gap between the A and the frame: the A is in front of the picture, not touching it.
- Leave clear space around the logo of at least the width of the A's leg.
- Don't stretch it, rotate it or recolour parts of it. For a single colour, use the ink or the white version.
- Below 48 px, use `piccab-favicon.svg`: the thin lines of the full icon blur at that size.
