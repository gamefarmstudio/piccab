Why learn from pictures

Why do words learned from a list
vanish when you need them?

A word learned with a picture and a place in a scene has something for your memory to hold on to. Review it at the right moments and you forget it far more slowly.

The usual way

✕Word lists & single-word flashcards

😕
Words without context

You can recite “whisk = Schneebesen”, but standing in a real kitchen it won't come — you've never seen it next to anything else.

🔁
Translating in your head every time

Foreign word → your language → picture. It's slow, and it never feels natural when you need it.

📉
Cram once, forget fast

Without review, most of what you learn fades within the first few days (Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve).

Result: lots of time memorising, little you can actually use
The Piccab way

✓Learn from real scenes

🖼️
Words tied to pictures and places

The teapot sits next to the cups and saucers, so your brain stores them as a set — recall one and the ones around it follow.

👀
See the thing, think the word

You practise from pictures, not translations, so you gradually stop translating in your head.

👂
Several senses at once

Eyes on the picture, ears on the sound, a finger to find it and typing to spell it — the same word from many directions.

Result: see the real thing and the word comes — and it stays

FAQ

Questions about Piccab

Do I need to sign up? Does it cost anything?

No sign-up needed. Open piccab.com/app and start with the sample scene, or make a scene from a photo. Your progress is kept on your device. Sign in with email or Google to sync across devices and get monthly AI credits for finding words in photos.

Every feature is free right now. In the future we may offer paid plans for extras such as more AI credits or tools for teachers and schools, while keeping basic practice free. We'll tell you before anything changes, and we'll never charge you unless you choose to subscribe (see Terms of Use, section 1.1).

Which languages can I learn?

English and German. Tap the flag button at the top of the app to switch. The same scenes work in both languages; German nouns show der/die/das in colour, and you can type ae/oe/ue/ss instead of ä/ö/ü/ß. Progress and reviews are kept separately for each language. More languages are on the way.

Can I use the app in my own language?

The menus are in English or Thai, and the meaning under each word follows that choice — for example, practising German with English menus shows the English word under each German one. Change it any time under the flag button → “My language”.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I start?

Yes. Every word has a meaning and a voice. Start in 👀 Explore words and tap things in the picture to hear them, then try typing and Listen & find. Hints help you one step at a time.

What devices does it work on? Does it work offline?

It runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, and you can install it on your home screen like an app. Once you've opened it, your scenes work without internet. AI and syncing need a connection.

Where do my photos go when I use AI? Is it safe?

Photos are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) only to find the words. If you're signed in, your photos and scenes are kept in your account and you can delete them at any time. Details are in the Privacy Policy. Please don't photograph people's faces or personal information.

Can children use it?

Yes — Piccab is made for every age. Children can practise without an account. In a class, students join with the class code and a nickname, no email needed, and student accounts don't use AI. Anyone under 20 should ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

How does spaced review work?

Words you've typed correctly come back after 1 day, then after 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 120 days as long as you answer without help. Use a hint or get it wrong and the word comes back sooner. There's nothing to set up.
